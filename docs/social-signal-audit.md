## Publication rule

Only platform-level conclusions explicitly supported by a mapped product radar record are published. Creator names, original posts, excerpts, and source URLs remain excluded. A platform with no useful product-level content is not shown. Records without a `SOCIAL MEDIA ECHO` section remain unchanged rather than being filled by inference.
